Yohaku Nagaya 余白長屋

Type Residential / Row House
Location 1-chōme-13-13 Momogaikechō, Abeno Ward, Osaka 545-0012, Japan
Frontage 5 m
Status In progress

A sundial teaches its time through shadows on a canvas of light. But is hard to interpret through overcast and forces of nature. 余白長屋 shows time through light on a canvas of shadows. The Narrow 5m row house has a gigantic void in the middle that funnels light, rain, nature down to its first floor piloti area, which casts a single square of light on the ground depending on the time and weather.
